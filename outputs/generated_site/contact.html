Frequently asked questions

How do I reach a person?
Open the chat on this page or in your app and type your question. An adviser picks it up. You can also ask for a call back or book an appointment at a branch.
How do I change my daily transfer limit?
You set your own limit in the app or on this site. Your limit is [MONTANT]. Change the amount, confirm with your usual code, and you can set it back the same way whenever you want.
I see a payment I do not recognise. What do I do?
Tell us through chat or a secure message and send us the payment details. If a card is involved, block it in the app first. We look into it with you and keep you informed in your messages.
Can I contact you about a mortgage or an investment?
Yes. Choose the topic in the chat and we route you to a colleague who works with mortgages or investments. You can also book an appointment at a branch.
When do I get an answer?
Chat and phone are answered during opening hours. A secure message usually gets an answer within [DUREE].
