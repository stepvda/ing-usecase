Frequently asked questions

Can I block my card myself?
Yes. Open the app, choose your card and block it. If you find it again you can unblock it yourself. If not, order a new one in the same screen and follow the delivery.
How quickly do I get an answer?
During opening hours an advisor picks up your chat. A secure message that arrives outside those hours is answered on the next banking day.
I do not live near a branch. Can I arrange everything remotely?
Yes. Opening an account, ordering a card and most changes happen in the app. If a step asks for something you cannot do on your phone, call us and we look for another way.
Where do I report a suspicious message or a fraudulent payment?
Report it in the app or call us. If money has already left your account, call straight away. Always use the number on the back of your card, never a number from the message itself.
Can someone else contact you on my behalf?
Only if you have given that person a mandate. We record it in your file, so they can act for you without repeating the situation every time.
