Frequently asked questions

Is my money safe with ING?
Your deposits are covered by the Belgian deposit guarantee scheme, up to [MONTANT] per person per bank. Logins and payments use strong authentication. The legal text on deposit protection is available from ING Belgium.
Can I still speak to a real person?
Yes. You reach an advisor by phone on working days, and you can book an appointment at a branch in Belgium. You find every number and address on the Contact page.
What do I need before I open an account?
Your identity card and your personal details. You complete one online form and confirm your identity as part of it. Nothing needs to be printed.
Does ING have branches in Belgium?
Yes. You can make an appointment at a branch near you for advice on an account, a saving plan or a mortgage, and you keep the app for your daily banking.
