Frequently asked questions

Is ING a Belgian bank?
Yes. ING Belgium has its head office in Brussels, and deposits held here are covered by the Belgian deposit protection scheme, up to [MONTANT] per depositor.
Can I do everything in the app?
Almost everything you do in a normal week: check balances, make payments, block a card, chat with an adviser. Some things, such as a mortgage application, are easier to do with a person, in a branch or on the phone.
What if I get stuck while opening an account?
Save and stop where you are. Call us or make an appointment, and an adviser picks up your application from that point. You do not start again from the beginning.
Where do I get help if something goes wrong on my account?
Report it in the app or call us. You speak to a person who can see your account and tell you what happens next.
