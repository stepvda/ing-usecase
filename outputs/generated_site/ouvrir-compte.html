What fast means, step by step

Fast is a word anyone can write. Here is the process in full, so you can judge it for yourself.

  • Step 1. Open the ING Banking app or ing.be and choose the account you want.
  • Step 2. Confirm who you are with itsme or with your ID card.
  • Step 3. Answer a short list of questions about your address and your income.
  • Step 4. Sign digitally. Your account number and the next steps follow by email.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to open an account?
Your Belgian ID card or the itsme app, a mobile number and an email address we can reach you on, your address and your income details. Keep about [DUREE] free for the questions.
How long does the application take?
The form asks a short list of questions and takes about [DUREE] to fill in. We do not make a stopwatch claim, because the time depends on the documents you have at hand.
Is the current account really free?
It is free when the conditions are met: your salary of at least [MONTANT] arrives each month, or you keep at least [MONTANT] in the account at the end of the month. If neither is met, the monthly fee is [MONTANT]. You see these conditions before you sign.
Can I stop halfway and come back later?
Yes. Your answers are kept, and you continue from the same step when you return. Nothing about your application expires while you are away.
Do I have to move my salary straight away?
No. You can open the account first and set up the salary transfer afterwards. If the account is free only when your salary arrives, we say so before you sign.
