Frequently asked questions

What rate does the Savings Account pay?
The Savings Account pays [TAUX] on your balance. The rate is shown with your account and is the same whether you open in the app, online or in a branch.
Does the current offer have an end date?
One offer runs at a time, and its end date is shown next to the rate. After that date, the standard rate applies.
Can I take my money out when I want?
Yes. On the Savings Account you can add or withdraw money whenever you like, from the app. There is no notice period.
What is the difference between the Savings Account and the Term Account?
The Savings Account keeps your money reachable at [TAUX]. The Term Account fixes your rate for [DUREE] and pays it at the end of that period.
How do I open a savings account?
Open it in the ING app or online, or ask at a branch. You need your eID and your account details, and you see the steps before you start.
