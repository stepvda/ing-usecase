Free means free

Free for your child means no monthly account fee, no minimum deposit and no salary transfer to arrange. Nothing has to be paid in for the account to stay free.

One condition applies: the account stays free while your child is studying. That is the whole qualification behind the word free, and you will find the same wording in the tariff sheet.

  • No monthly account fee.
  • No minimum deposit to keep the account open.
  • No salary transfer, no direct debit you have to set up.
  • Free for as long as your child is studying.

Open it from your phone, step by step

You open the account yourself in the ING app. Choose the youth account, add your child's details and sign with itsme. The account number appears in the app straight away.

Your child's card follows by post within [DUREE]. Until it arrives, the balance is already visible on your phone and on theirs. Nothing has to be printed or posted back.

What your child gets

Your child gets a debit card in their own name and the ING app on their own device. You keep the controls: the card limit, the pause button and a clear view of the balance.

There is also a savings pot in the app, so a birthday present or a first pay slip has somewhere to go.

  • A debit card in your child's name, with a spending limit you set.
  • The balance and every payment, visible in your app and in theirs.

Frequently asked questions

Who opens the account, and whose name is on it?
A parent or legal guardian opens it in the ING app and signs as the representative. The account is in your child's name, and you keep access and control until your child takes it over.
Is the account really free?
Yes, while your child is studying. No monthly account fee, no minimum deposit, no salary transfer. If that ever changes, ING writes to you before the new condition starts, with the date it applies from.
What do I need at hand when I open it?
Your ING app or card reader, your child's national register number and ID, and your own ID. You sign with itsme. Nothing has to be printed or sent by post.
Can my child spend with the card straight away?
As soon as the card arrives and is activated in the app. The limit on the card is the one you set, and you can pause the card from your own app at any time.
What happens when my child stops studying?
The conditions of the account change at that point. ING contacts you in advance so you can decide what comes next, in your own time.
