Frequently asked questions

Can I lose money if I invest?
Yes. Investments can fall as well as rise, and you can get back less than you paid in. That is why the risk warning sits with the offer rather than at the foot of the page.
How much do I need to start?
You can start from [MONTANT]. After that, you decide what you add and when.
How long should I stay invested?
Think in a horizon of [DUREE]. Money you may need before then is better kept in a savings account.
What will it cost?
You see the costs before you confirm any investment, next to the amount you enter. Nothing is added later without you seeing it.
Do I have to decide before a deadline?
No. There is no deadline on an investment account. If an offer ever ends on a set date, we give that date once, in plain words.
