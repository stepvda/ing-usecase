What you need to know before you invest

Investing is not a savings account. The value of what you hold can fall as well as rise, and you can get back less than you put in.

Past results do not tell you what happens next. Invest money you will not need in the short term, and ask us if you are unsure what suits you.

  • Your investment can lose value.
  • You may get back less than you paid in.
  • Costs apply whether your investment rises or falls.
  • Past performance does not predict future results.
