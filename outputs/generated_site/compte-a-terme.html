Frequently asked questions

Is the rate really fixed for the whole term?
Yes. The rate you confirm at the start, [TAUX], applies for the full term of [DUREE]. It does not move up or down while your money is in the account.
Can I take my money out before the term ends?
Your money is meant to stay for the full term. Withdrawing earlier is possible under specific conditions: [conditions apply]. Ask us before you deposit so you know exactly what applies to your situation.
How does a term account compare with a savings account?
A savings account keeps your money reachable and the rate can change. A term account fixes both your money and your rate for the term you choose. If you will not need the amount for a while, the fixed rate is the reason to choose it.
What happens when the term ends?
You decide what comes next: renew for a new term at the rate available then, move the money to a savings account, or take it out. We get in touch before the end date so you can choose without rushing.
How do I open a term account?
Choose your amount and term, confirm the rate shown to you, and transfer the amount. You can follow the account in the app once it is open.
