Term account or savings account

A savings account keeps your money reachable. Its rate can move up or down, because it follows the market.

A term account pays a rate you fix in advance for a set period. You give up some flexibility and you get a rate that does not move.

If you may need the money soon, a savings account fits better. If you know you will leave a sum alone for [DUREE], a term account gives you the certainty of [TAUX] a year.

  • Reachable at any time: savings account
  • Fixed rate for a set period: term account
  • Rate known before you start: term account
