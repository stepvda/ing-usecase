From the first appointment to the keys

The first appointment takes about an hour, in a branch or by video. Bring your ID, your last payslips or your most recent tax assessment, an overview of your existing loans and the details of the property you have in mind. We look at your income, your current credits and the value of the property, and we tell you what is possible.

You then receive a written offer with the rate, the term, the monthly repayment and the total cost of the loan. Your adviser explains each line, answers what is still unclear, and you take the signed offer to the notary for the deed. Plan about [DUREE] between the signed offer and the signing at the notary.

Frequently asked questions

How much can I borrow?
That depends on your income, your existing loans and the value of the property. We work it out from your own figures at the first appointment and put the amount in writing. The example on this page shows up to [MONTANT].
What does the TAEG include?
The TAEG brings the interest rate and the costs of the loan, such as the file fee and the insurance, into one yearly figure so you can compare offers. Representative example: TAEG [TAUX]. The figure for your own file appears in your written offer.
Which documents do I bring to the first appointment?
Your ID, your last payslips or your most recent tax assessment, an overview of your existing loans and credits, and the address or listing of the property you have in mind.
Can I repay early?
In most cases, yes. The conditions and any compensation are set out in your offer, and your adviser explains them before you sign. Ask about it at the appointment so you know where you stand.
