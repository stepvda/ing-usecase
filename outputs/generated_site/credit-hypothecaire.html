Frequently asked questions

What does the TAEG mean?
It is the total cost of your mortgage in one figure, including the interest and the costs of the loan. You see your own TAEG in your written offer.
Is my rate fixed?
Yes, for the term you choose. The rate and the term are confirmed in writing before you sign.
How much can I borrow?
That depends on your income, your file and the property. At the appointment we tell you what is possible and put it in writing.
Can I repay early?
Ask your adviser about repaying early. The conditions that apply to your loan are in your offer, and we explain them before you sign.
What do I bring to the first appointment?
Your ID, proof of your income and the information you have about the property. If something is missing, tell us and we say what else we need.
